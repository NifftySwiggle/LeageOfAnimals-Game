# 🦅 League of Animals (LOA)

[![Play Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play%20Now-GitHub%20Pages-00f0ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://nifftyswiggle.github.io/LOA/)
[![Tech Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Made%20With-Three.js%20%7C%20WebGL-ff0055?style=for-the-badge)](https://threejs.org/)

**League of Animals (LOA)** is an open-world 3D superhero action game that runs directly in your browser. Choose your hero, explore Metropolis, swing across skyscrapers, fly with repulsor jets, drive cars, defeat enemy syndicates, and collect hidden golden artifacts!

👉 **[Click Here to Play the Game Live](https://nifftyswiggle.github.io/LOA/)**

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Choose Your Hero**: Pick **Spider-Ram** for high-flying web swinging and parkour, or **Iron-Ram** for supersonic jet flight and explosive repulsor cannons.
2. **Explore Metropolis**: Roam freely across the open city, scale skyscrapers, or hijack civilian cars to cruise the streets.
3. **Fight Crime Incursions**: Follow red threat markers to locate enemy syndicate portals and defeat hostile waves.
4. **Hunt Collectibles**: Search rooftops, hidden alleys, and high towers to find all 15 Golden Bighorn Artifacts.
5. **Unlock Achievements**: Complete challenges to unlock all 18 in-game trophies.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Control |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` |
| **Look Around** | `Mouse` |
| **Attack / Shoot** | `Left Click` |
| **Web Swing / Jet Flight** | `Hold Right Click` |
| **Jump / Double Jump Flip** | `Spacebar` |
| **Dodge / Roll Dash** | `Left Shift` |
| **Web Stun Blast** | `E` |
| **Wall Climb** | `Q` |
| **Enter / Drive Car** | `F` |
| **Toggle Camera (1st/3rd View)** | `V` |
| **Hold Tactical GPS Map** | `Hold M` |
| **Pause & Settings** | `ESC` |

> *Controls can be customized anytime in the in-game settings.*

## 🦸 Heroes

### 🐏 Spider-Ram
- **Web Swinging**: Hold right-click while looking at tall buildings to attach webs and swing with real momentum.
- **Wall Climbing**: Cling to buildings and climb up walls.
- **Web Blaster**: Shoot webs to temporarily freeze enemies.
- **Ram Combo**: 3-hit melee strike to knock back foes.

### 🛡️ Iron-Ram
- **Jet Thrusters**: Hold right-click to fly and hover in any direction.
- **Plasma Cannons**: Left-click to shoot explosive energy bolts.
- **Chest Uni-Beam**: Fire a high-powered sustained energy beam.
- **Ground Slam**: Smash down from high altitude to trigger a radial shockwave.
